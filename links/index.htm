Links are listed below to a number of sites that may be of interest to those who would like further information on any of the subjects covered on this site.

WIGGS is a One Name Society (Wigglesworth International Genealogy Group Study) a focus for those researching that name and has much data and many family trees to help members.  It meets once a year and publishes newsletters.  Anne Newman at Buckden is the leading light.

The village of Wigglesworth has a website, click here to find out about modern day Wigglesworth.

Malhamdale Local History pages offers interesting information on the area.
