Life at Matlock College

Rockside 1960s

Matlock College was set up just after the second World War to train teachers and its development is summarised here, the increasing numbers, the increasingly academic courses and education for teachers beyond that. There are photographs and personal memories of events during its forty years or so. The list of references directs readers to the few documentary sources.

To download Life at Matlock College it is in two parts, right click here part one and select "Save Target As" for part one and right click here part two and select "Save Target As" for part two.

A second booklet, Matlock College in Retrospect, is intended to fill in some of the background of the life at the college detailed in the first with emphasis on the academic and professional aspects by recording some personal thoughts of those involved at the time.
