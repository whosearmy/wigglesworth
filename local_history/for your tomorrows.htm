Christ Church, Holloway
Village War Memorial
There are seven war memorials in the villages of Dethick, Lea and Holloway and these researches were prompted by a villager remarking that "although we see the names on the wall in church every Sunday they mean less and less as time passes."  The early editions of the Parish Magazine and the memories of older villagers add personal recollections to what might otherwise be a sterile account.  This 2007 edition adds information from the National War Memorial web site http://www.ukniwm.org.uk/server/ to the 1991 booklet.
