Matlock Bridge
(C18)

Having moved to Lums Hill Rise in Matlock, Derbyshire, I have been curious to know the history of this limited area between the turnpike and Asker Lane and the derivation of this name. It is interesting that the land was once owned by Peter Nightingale (1737-1803) borders a turnpike and, nearby, lead was transported and smelted, for all those facts were true of our previous house in Lea Bridge, near Matlock.

Asker Lane i & ii were originally combined but grew until they needed to be split.

Asker i is mainly focused on more recent times. To download the Asker Lane i right click here download file and select "Save Target As".

Asker Lane ii records earlier features of this area on the northern edge of Matlock, Derbyshire. Much of it deals with a rather confused picture but gathers together some of the evidence and argument.
